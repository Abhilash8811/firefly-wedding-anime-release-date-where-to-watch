## Firefly Wedding Release Date

The Firefly Wedding anime premiered on **Friday, October 9, 2026** on Fuji TV's Noitamina programming block in Japan. Fuji TV lists the first broadcast at 11:30 p.m. Japan time.

A few earlier screenings came first:

- **Anime Expo:** the first episode was shown on July 4.
- **Japan premiere event:** a screening of the first three episodes was scheduled for September 6, with a talk featuring the lead voice actors.

The anime was first announced in January 2026.

> **Note:** Crunchyroll's exact release time for your region can differ from Japan's TV schedule. Check the service for the time in your area rather than converting the Japanese broadcast time yourself.

## What Is Firefly Wedding About?

Satoko Kirigaya is a young noblewoman in Meiji-era Japan with a heart condition. She wants to secure a good marriage for her family before she dies. While out in the city, she is kidnapped by hired thugs and handed to Shinpei Gotō, an assassin who has been paid to execute her.

In a desperate attempt to survive, Satoko proposes marriage and claims it is love at first sight. Shinpei accepts, and what began as a bluff turns into a dangerous bond as the two escape together. The story is a mix of romance, danger, and obsession.

If you want to know how the story ends, see the spoiler section of the manga guide below.

**Spin-offs:** after the main series ended, Manga ONE released side stories, including *Koi to Doku* (Love and Poison) and *Hotaru no Yomeiri Gaiden: Hitokiri to Yōdō* (The Assassin and a Young Child).

### Spoiler: how does the manga end?

*Skip this if you are watching the anime blind.*

The manga builds toward Satoko's death, with her condition worsening and her being told she has about a month left. The final chapters then jump forward decades. An elderly Shinpei returns to their old home on Tennyo Island, which is about to be demolished, and finds Satoko still waiting for him. They reunite as elders. The ending is **bittersweet** and is one of the most debated parts of the series.

## Should You Watch It First or Read the Manga?

- **Watch first** if you like discovering a story through voice acting, music, and animation, and want to avoid spoilers.
- **Read first** if you want the full story, since a single season will not cover all 12 volumes.
- **Do both** if you enjoy comparing adaptations. The manga is complete, so you can read it at your own pace.

## Who Is This Anime For?

Firefly Wedding is a good fit if you enjoy:

- Historical romance with a dark, dangerous edge
- Stories where a fake relationship becomes real
- Slow-burn emotional drama with a bittersweet tone

It may not suit you if you prefer light-hearted romance, since the story deals with illness, violence, and obsession.
